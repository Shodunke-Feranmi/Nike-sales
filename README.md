# Nike Sales Dashboard
 
A Power BI Project analyzing Nike's regional profitability, monthly profit trends, sales channel revenue, and product performance across 2023–2025.
 
---
 
## 📌 Project Overview
 
This project analyzes Nike sales data across Indian cities, tracking profit by region, monthly profit trends, revenue by sales channel (Online vs. Retail), best-selling products, and year-over-year unit sales. The dashboard is interactive, filterable by **Year** and **Gender Category** (Kids, Men, Women).
 
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Years Covered | 2023–2025 |
| Total Revenue (Online + Retail) | ₹427.51K |
| Total Regional Profit (7 cities) | ₹552K |
| Top Region by Profit | Mumbai (₹115K) |
 
## 🎯 Business Problem
 
A retail brand needs to know which regions and products are actually driving profit, whether Online or Retail is the stronger channel, and whether sales are trending up or down month to month, so investment (inventory, marketing, store presence) can be directed where it has the most impact.
 
## 📊 Project Objectives
 
- Compare profit across regions to identify top and underperforming markets
- Track monthly profit trend to spot growth or seasonal patterns
- Compare revenue contribution from Online vs. Retail channels
- Identify the best-selling products by units sold
- Compare yearly unit sales across 2023, 2024, and 2025

 
## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**
  - Data modeling
  - Column, bar, line, and donut charts
  - Interactive slicers (Year, Gender Category)
  - Trend and channel comparison analysis
## 📈 Key Findings
 
- **Mumbai leads all regions in profit** (₹115K), followed closely by Kolkata (₹110K) and Delhi (₹95K). The seven listed regions combine for **₹552K in total profit**.
- **Monthly profit shows a clear upward trend**, more than doubling from ₹30K in August to ₹72K in November — a 140% increase across the period shown.
- **Retail slightly outsells Online**: ₹225.89K (52.84%) from Retail versus ₹201.62K (47.16%) from Online, out of ₹427.51K total revenue. The two channels are close, but Retail has the edge.
- **Dunk and Flex Trainer are the top-selling products**, each with 56 units sold, followed closely by a cluster of products in the 40–46 unit range; **Kyrie Flytrap is the lowest seller** shown, at 14 units.
- **2024 was the strongest year by units sold** (338), narrowly ahead of 2025 (332), while **2023 shows only 51 units** — likely a partial year of data rather than a real decline, since the jump to 2024's full-year total is too large to reflect a real trend.
## 💡 Business Insights
 
1. **Profit is concentrated in a few strong regions.** Mumbai, Kolkata, and Delhi together account for over half of total regional profit (320K of 552K), while Bangalore/Bengaluru and Pune trail well behind. Expansion or marketing investment is likely to pay off fastest by strengthening the existing top three rather than spreading resources evenly.
2. **Growth is real and recent.** The November figure (72K) is more than double August's (30K), and the trend climbs steadily rather than spiking once, which is a healthier signal than a single good month. Worth investigating what changed starting around January/February to sustain and replicate it.
3. **Retail and Online are close enough that both channels deserve continued investment.** With only a 5.7-point gap between them (52.84% vs. 47.16%), this isn't a case of one channel clearly winning — a channel-specific strategy shift should be backed by more than this current split alone.
4. **A small group of products (Dunk, Flex Trainer, and the next few) drive the bulk of unit sales.** Inventory and marketing priority should follow this ranking rather than treating the full product catalog equally.
